Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

All standard bookings feature free cancellation with 100% refund up to 7 days before check-in. In case of weather emergencies or travel disruptions, our concierge team works directly with hosts to reschedule without penalty.

Are pets welcome at SYLVAN farmsteads?

Many properties in our collection are pet-friendly, especially farmhouses and cottages with enclosed garden acreage. Simply select the "Pet-Friendly" filter on our Stays page to view participating accommodations.

What criteria must a host meet to list a property?

Properties must demonstrate authentic connection to the land (working farms, orchards, vineyard acreage, or secluded natural sanctuaries), high architectural standards, and eco-conscious waste and energy practices.
